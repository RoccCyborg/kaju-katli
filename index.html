About the birthday star

Kanishka, also known as Kaju Katli

A name that sounds like a celebration. Here is why it fits, and what this galaxy wishes for her.

The name

Kaju katli is the diamond-shaped cashew sweet that shows up at every festival, often wearing a thin sheet of edible silver. Rich, smooth, and gone from the plate faster than anyone expected. Some names are a compliment on their own.

The date

23 November 2026. The countdown on the Home page runs to midnight IST, so it ends at the same instant for everyone, in every time zone.
